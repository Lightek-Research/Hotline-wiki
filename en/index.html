HOTLINE

Record GLights commands. Build a timeline. Play your show.

HOTLINE is a timeline editor for GLights in Roblox. Arrange lighting commands into cues, record their timing, and replay a show with optional music.

Choose Timed to record a sequence as the clock runs, or Capture to wait for one lighting command and store it at the beginning of a cue.

Start here

  1. Install and configure HOTLINE.

  2. Record your first cue.

  3. Arrange cues on the timeline.

  4. Save and play your show.

Developers can also read Configuration and Security and performance. If something does not work, see Troubleshooting.

Key terms

Term

Meaning

Show

A project containing cues, commands, playback settings, and optional music.

Cue

A container placed at a specific time and track. It contains lighting events.

Event / command

A recorded GLights action, such as turning a target on or off.

Track

A visual row for organizing cues. It does not isolate the lighting hardware.

Operator

The authorized player who owns the current editor session.

Cue time is relative to the show. Event time is relative to its cue. An event at 1.5 s inside a cue starting at 10 s is scheduled at 11.5 s in the show.

This guide covers HOTLINE 0.14.0. Captions and icons may differ in customized interfaces.

New features

  • Edit / Load and the compact playback toolbar.
  • Multi-selection, colors, groups and expandable Cue List.
  • Smooth Follow, four layouts and customizable shortcuts.
  • Timed / Capture with Append / Replace.
  • Optional music, Workspace Sound links and automatic stop.
  • All 46 controls with updated illustrations.
