HOTLINE / Timeline and Cue List

Timeline and Cue List

Place cues on tracks and multi-select with Ctrl+click or Shift+click. Event offsets are relative to their source cue. See the interface reference for every control. Interface reference.

Automatic timeline length

The timeline expands as you record, place cues or navigate to later times. The initial duration stored for an empty show does not limit the cursor to one second. Creating a show does not require a fixed maximum duration. When music is used, automatic stop at the track end is independent of the workspace length.

Multi-selection, tracks and colors

Use Ctrl+click or Shift+click to add or remove cues from the selection. Dragging a selected cue moves the whole selection with its relative offsets preserved. Clone and Delete apply to the selection. Dragging a right edge resizes only that cue.

New cues use the first free track for their time interval. If the first eight tracks are occupied, later tracks are used. + Track adds a track manually. Set the limit with MaxTracks in Config; its default is 128.

Color applies a palette color or six-digit RGB HEX, such as 338DAC, to the selection. Cue colors are saved with the show and copied with cues. They do not send fixture color commands.

Group and Ungroup

Select at least two cues and press Group / Ctrl+G. They combine into one group while preserving command execution times.

Ungroup / Ctrl+U restores the component cues of new groups, including names, colors, durations and relative time / track offsets. Moving a group moves the restored cues with it. New commands recorded directly into a group become an additional cue. Deleted or overwritten events are not restored from an old snapshot. Undo can revert grouping or ungrouping within the current session.

Older groups created before membership metadata was saved do not contain the original cue boundaries. They can be split into commands while preserving timing; the old group's name must end with group. Original names and durations cannot be recovered without saved metadata.

Groups in Cue List

Existing groups appear as separate rows with a lighter outline.

  1. Click [+] or the group row to show its component cues.
  2. Click a component cue to show its commands.
  3. Click again to collapse that level.

Expansion is visual only. It does not invoke Group / Ungroup, alter saved cues or reorder playback commands. Expansion works in Load too, while command-editing buttons remain hidden.

The list uses pages and reusable rows rather than allocating thousands of GUI objects at once. The current position has a green outline. LAST and NEXT identify schedule positions, not an execution acknowledgement from fixtures.

Smooth Follow

Follow is available in More and on the compact Load toolbar. Both buttons share one ON / OFF state.

In Timeline, Follow moves the viewport smoothly with the playhead. In Cue List, it scrolls smoothly to the current position and changes pages when needed. Scrolling animation does not affect the playback clock or command order.

Cue List Previous / Next turns Follow off so you can inspect other pages. Press Follow again to resume automatic following. Automatic scrolling stops while playback is paused.

Editing during playback

Edit mode supports renaming, moving, recoloring, duplicating, deleting and grouping cues during playback. The new schedule applies to future commands. Past commands are not replayed, and deleting an already executed command does not reverse its effect on fixtures.

Pause playback and finish recording before Save / Load. Use Save to keep editor changes beyond the current session.

Window and scale

Four layouts are available: Large, Wide, Tall and Compact. Drag the window using any edge, its title bar or free background. Buttons, fields, cues and scrollable workspaces keep their normal actions.

The supplied GUI scale is 0.9. To change it in Studio with Play stopped, open ServerScriptService → Hotline → Scripts → HotlineGui → Window → Scale and change Scale. GUI scale is separate from the − / + timeline zoom controls.
